# ✨ BirthVerse — Your Birthday Story & World History Explorer

A creative Python web application where users enter their name and birthday to discover:

1. **⏱️ Live Life Clock**:
   - Exact calendar age (Years, Months, Days)
   - Live-ticking **Total Days Lived**, **Total Hours Lived**, **Total Minutes**, and **Real-Time Seconds Alive**
   - Next birthday countdown, leap days experienced, and day-of-the-week birth lore
2. **🌍 On This Day in World History & Famous Birthday Twins**:
   - Hybrid historical engine that combines live Wikimedia "On This Day" chronicles (when online) with a rich built-in curated archive of world history, science/space breakthroughs, pop culture moments, and famous shared birthdays
3. **📻 Birth Year Cultural Time Capsule**:
   - World population when you were born vs. how many billion humans have joined Earth since your birth
   - Technology, music, and cinema of your birth era + vintage price tags (movie ticket, gallon of fuel, ounce of gold)
4. **💓 Live Biological & Cosmic Odyssey**:
   - Real-time ticking **heartbeats**, **breaths taken**, **eye blinks**, **hours spent sleeping & dreams had**
   - **Cosmic Speedometer**: How many millions/billions of kilometers you have traveled orbiting the Sun (`29.78 km/s`) and moving through the Milky Way Galaxy (`230 km/s`)
5. **🪐 Astronomical Birth Chart & Solar System Ages**:
   - Dynamic **SVG Moon Phase** illustration showing the exact lunar illumination on the night you were born
   - **Western Zodiac**, **Chinese Zodiac (Element + Animal)**, and **Birthstone & Birth Flower**
   - **Planetary Age Calculator**: Your age and next birthday date on Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
6. **🏆 Landmark Temporal Milestones & Printable Life Summary Card**:
   - Exact dates for your **10,000th Day on Earth**, **1 Billionth Second Alive ($10^9\text{ s}$)**, **100,000th / 250,000th / 500,000th Hour**, and a printable/copyable **Official Birthday & Life Summary Card**.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

No external dependencies are required—runs out-of-the-box with Python 3!

```bash
python3 app.py
```

Then open **http://127.0.0.1:8080** in your browser.
